# What changed in the recheck (13 Nov 2026)

The archive was extended to today and every novel character was rechecked against the data itself: for each name,
what do the posts and comments say it is from? This file lists everything that came out differently from the
previous version of the lists.

## 1. Coverage

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Last post / comment in the archive | 3 Nov 2026 | **13 Nov 2026** |
| Posts | 44,007 | **44,474** (+467) |
| Comments | 704,066 | **710,836** (+6,770) |

- The archive in the repository stopped on **3 Nov 2026**, not 11 Nov. The missing ten days were downloaded and
  appended; no duplicates were created (checked by id).
- **Start date:** the subreddit's first post is from **10 Nov 2023**. The archive source was queried for anything
  earlier and returned nothing, so 1 to 9 Nov 2023 is empty because the subreddit did not exist yet.
- No novel character appears for the first time in the new ten days; the new data only adds mentions of
  characters that were already known.

## 7. How the recheck works

- Every character-to-novel link in `analysis/novel_catalog.txt` was checked by reading what the archive says
  about the name ("X from Y", "X (Y)", "the MC of Y"), and by measuring which novel each name is mentioned next to.
- Names that are also ordinary words or shared with other series (Adam, Amon, Kirito, Shiro, Lynch, Kei ...) are
  counted only when their novel is named in the same post or comment, or in the post a comment belongs to.
- Longer names win over shorter ones, so "Hei Lou Lan" is never counted as "Lou Lan" and "Gu Yue Fang Yuan" is
  never counted as the Gu Yue clan.
- `results/novel_characters_evidence.csv` holds the numbers behind every entry: mentions, first and last date, and
  how often the novel is named alongside.
