## Method and caveats

- Source: the Arctic Shift Reddit archive, scraped with `scraper/scrape_subreddit.py`. Text covered: post titles, post bodies and every comment. **Text inside images is not read.** About 31,000 of the 44,000 posts are images or galleries, and OCR could not be installed on the machine used.
- `analysis/discover_entities.py` scans all text for capitalized names, "X vs Y" title sides and "Name (Work)" tags, then counts each name case-insensitively. A post or comment counts once per character.
- Character-to-work labels come from a hand-checked dictionary (`analysis/analyze_mentions.py`, `analysis/reviewed_names.txt`). Names marked *(unidentified ...)* have no confirmed source, and *(ambiguous)* names are shared by several characters.
- Short or ambiguous names (e.g. *Light*, *Kira*, *Hal*) are matched only when capitalised, so lowercase uses are missed and a few unrelated uses are caught. Treat counts as approximate.
- An arc's work is inferred from the characters mentioned alongside it, and left blank when no work clearly dominates.
